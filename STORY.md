# Snowflake Atelier: Story

**Premise.** You are the new apprentice at the Atelier of Madame Isolde Hoarfrost, the winter paper-artist. The Gallery of Winter opens at the end of the week. Every piece must be cut from ice-paper, one careful step at a time, and every piece is sewn round with a golden measuring thread.

## Characters
- **Madame Isolde Hoarfrost**: the artist. Precise, dry and secretly delighted by infinity. She teaches with one rule: "Do the same thing again, however small."
- **Pim the ermine**: the Atelier's other apprentice, and a borrower of scissors. He asks the questions a kid would ask ("Does it get as big as the room?") and gets the jokes.
- **Lord Quillby**: an owl and the Gallery's patron. He pays for thread by the metre and counts every edge twice. He supplies the commissions: exact edge counts, budgets and scissor limits. Those are the "stop at the right depth" puzzles.

## Arc (5 chapters, 20 commissions)
1. **The First Fold**: a triangle learns a trick. Iteration, counting edges, and spotting self-similarity. The flake gets its name: Koch, 1904.
2. **The Endless Edge**: the golden thread. ×4/3 per cut, predicting thread length, and the big reveal: infinite edge, finite area (8/5 of the triangle). "Do not tell Lord Quillby. He will faint."
3. **Commissions**: Quillby's orders. 48 edges, a 64-coin thread budget, 768 edges, and a 1 mm scissor limit. The lesson is knowing when to stop.
4. **Other Weathers**: Pim pushes the bumps the wrong way and invents the anti-snowflake. Then come shy 30° frost and a "modern" square island for Quillby's cousin.
5. **Holes & Dust**: fractals made by taking away (Sierpinski lace, Cantor's dust), then opening night with the step-6 snowflake of 12,288 edges in the same circle. Isolde hands over the studio. Pim asks to keep the scissors.

Tone: warm, a little dry, never condescending. Every number said in dialogue is checked by a unit test (`tests/unit/levels.test.ts`).
